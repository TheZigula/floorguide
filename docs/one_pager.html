FloorGuide: the right answer
from the right binder.

For floor supervisors. Ask in plain words; get the answer from the safety procedure, the maintenance manual, or the quality standard, with the page it came from, and a work order written up when a machine is due.

Safety procedures Maintenance manuals Quality standards

1The problem

When a machine acts up on Line 3, Dana has three binders that might hold the answer. Today she walks to the office, guesses which binder applies, or asks whoever is nearby.

The quick card on the wall and the manual in the drawer do not always agree, and nothing tells her which one wins. A wrong guess on a safety step is not a paperwork problem.

2What this does for you

  • Picks the right binder for the question and answers from it, showing the document, the passage, and which kind of source it was.
  • When two documents disagree, says which one governs and which one is out of date, instead of splitting the difference.
  • Works out whether a machine is due for service from its meter readings and the manual's interval, by fixed arithmetic, never a guess. A missing or backwards reading gets a plain refusal that names what is wrong.
  • When service is due, writes the work order for you: machine, task, priority, and the manual section behind it.

3What you do now

  • Ask the question the way you would ask a colleague.
  • Read the answer and the source it came from.
  • If a work order was drafted, read it and press Approve or Reject.
  • Tell it once which line you are on. It remembers next time.

4What stays in your control

  • Nothing is filed, sent, or changed without your Approve. Reject throws the draft away.
  • It will never tell you to skip a safety step, and it cannot approve anything itself.
  • A document that tries to give it orders is thrown out before it is read, and the screen says so.
  • Every answer was graded against an answer key before you saw this page, and the grading fails on purpose if a wrong answer slips through.
