# SP-05 Confined Space Entry (Press Pit and Chip Trench)

Northbridge Fabrication, Plant 2. The permit-required confined spaces on Line 3
are the press pit under hydraulic press P-102 and the chip trench that runs under
conveyor C-7. Both collect hydraulic oil mist, coolant vapour, and chips, and
neither has a second way out.

## 1. No entry without a permit

Entry means any part of the body past the plane of the opening, including
reaching in to clear a jam or to pick up a dropped tool. A permit is issued by
the line supervisor for that shift, for that space, for that task.

## 2. Before entry

1. Lockout/tagout under SP-01 on every machine that discharges into the space,
   including P-102 and C-7.
2. Test the atmosphere at the top, the middle, and the bottom of the space:
   oxygen, flammable vapour, carbon monoxide. Record the readings on the permit.
3. Ventilate and re-test if any reading is out of range. A space that will not
   come into range is not entered.
4. Set up the tripod and retrieval line for the pit. Harness on before the
   entrant is over the opening.

## 3. During entry

An attendant stays at the opening for the whole entry, does not take on any other
task, keeps the entrant in sight or in constant voice contact, and does not enter
the space. If the entrant stops answering, the attendant calls for rescue from
outside and starts non-entry retrieval. The attendant never goes in after them.

## 4. Ending the entry

The entrant leaves, tools are counted out, the opening is covered or barricaded,
and the permit is closed and returned to the supervisor the same shift. An open
permit at shift change is handed over in person.
