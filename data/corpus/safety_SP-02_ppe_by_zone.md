# SP-02 Personal Protective Equipment by Zone

Northbridge Fabrication, Plant 2. PPE is worn by everyone in the zone, including
visitors and contractors.

## Zone 1 - Line 3 floor (general)

Safety glasses with side shields. Metatarsal safety boots. Hearing protection
where posted. Long sleeves near hot work. Loose clothing, lanyards, rings, and
wrist watches are not worn on the floor.

## Zone 2 - Press cell, hydraulic press P-102

Everything in Zone 1, plus hearing protection at all times (the cell is posted
above 85 dBA while the pump runs), plus cut-resistant gloves when handling blanks
and finished brackets. A face shield over safety glasses is worn for any task
that opens a hydraulic line, changes the filter, or drains oil.

## Zone 3 - CNC mill M-31

Everything in Zone 1, plus eye protection rated for coolant mist. Gloves are NOT
worn at the spindle or at any rotating tool; a glove that catches will pull the
hand in. Gloves are worn for deburring at the bench only.

## Zone 4 - Conveyor C-7 and the chip trench

Everything in Zone 1. Entry into the trench is a confined space entry (SP-05).

## Damaged PPE

Scratched lenses, cracked shields, torn gloves, and boots with exposed steel are
taken out of use the same shift and replaced from the crib.
