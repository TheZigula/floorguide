# MM-M31 CNC Mill M-31 Maintenance Manual

Northbridge Fabrication, Plant 2, Line 3. Rev A, issued 2026-07-19. M-31 machines
the B-40 bracket after the press operation.

## 2. Service intervals

Intervals are in spindle running hours from the meter reading at the last service
of that item.

| Item | Interval | Reference |
|------|----------|-----------|
| Spindle lubrication | every 400 spindle hours | Sec 2.4 |
| Coolant, test and replace | every 750 spindle hours | Sec 2.6 |
| Way lube reservoir, top up | weekly | Sec 2.7 |
| Ball screw backlash, measure | every 2000 spindle hours | Sec 2.9 |

### 2.4 Spindle lubrication

Lubricate the spindle every 400 spindle hours with the specified oil only. A
spindle run dry is a head rebuild. Gloves are not worn at the spindle (SP-02).

### 2.6 Coolant

Test the concentration weekly with the refractometer. Replace the charge every
750 spindle hours, or sooner if the coolant smells or the pH falls below 8.5.
Spent coolant goes to the waste drum, never to a floor drain (SP-06).

## 6. Hour meter and the service log

The spindle hour meter counts spindle running hours only. When a spindle head or
an hour meter is replaced, the new meter starts at zero and the service log is
re-based the same day: record the old reading, the new reading, and the date.

A meter reading that is LOWER than the reading recorded at the last service is a
data error, not a machine that ran backwards. It means the meter was replaced and
the log was not re-based, the wrong machine was read, or the figure was typed
wrong. Service is never scheduled from a reading like that. The reading is
corrected in the service log first, by the technician who took it, and the
interval is then recomputed from the corrected figure.
