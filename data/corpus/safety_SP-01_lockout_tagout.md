# SP-01 Lockout/Tagout for Line 3 Equipment

Northbridge Fabrication, Plant 2. Applies to every machine on Line 3, including
hydraulic press P-102, conveyor C-7, and CNC mill M-31. This procedure governs.
No maintenance manual, card, or tip sheet may set it aside.

## 1. When lockout is required

Lockout/tagout is required before any service, cleaning, jam clearing, filter
change, guard removal, or work that places any part of the body inside a guarded
area. It is also required after an emergency stop or an interlock trip before the
guarded area is entered (see SP-03).

## 2. Steps

1. Tell the line supervisor and the operators on the affected machine that the
   machine is coming down, and note the time on the shift board.
2. Shut the machine down at its own control, using the normal stopping sequence.
3. Isolate every energy source: open the electrical disconnect at the machine
   panel; close and lock the pneumatic supply valve; close the hydraulic supply
   valve.
4. Release stored energy. On P-102 this means bleeding the hydraulic accumulator
   down to zero at the bleed valve and confirming zero on the gauge, and blocking
   the ram or lowering it to the bed. Stored hydraulic pressure has moved a ram
   with the pump off.
5. Apply your own lock and your own tag to each isolation point. One worker, one
   lock. A lock is never applied, moved, or removed by anyone other than the
   worker whose name is on the tag.
6. Verify zero energy before touching anything: attempt a normal start at the
   machine control and confirm nothing moves, then return the control to off.
   Check the pressure gauge reads zero. This try-out step is not optional and is
   not satisfied by the panel light being dark.

## 3. Returning the machine to service

Remove tools, refit every guard, check that the guarded area is clear of people,
then each worker removes their own lock and tag. The machine is restarted only
after the line supervisor signs the restart entry in the shift log (SP-03).

## 4. Stop-work conditions

A missing lock, a shared lock, a tag without a name, a guard that will not close,
or a defeated interlock is a stop-work condition. Stop, leave the energy isolated,
and raise it with the line supervisor.
