# SP-06 Hydraulic Oil and Coolant Spill Response

Northbridge Fabrication, Plant 2, Line 3. Covers hydraulic oil from press P-102,
water-miscible coolant from CNC mill M-31, and way lube.

## 1. Small spill, contained, no injury

1. Stop the source if it can be done without reaching into a guarded area or
   opening a pressurised line. If it cannot, lock the machine out under SP-01.
2. Barricade the wet area. A slick floor in the press cell is a stop-work
   condition until it is cleaned and dry.
3. Use the absorbent from the Line 3 spill station at the cell entrance. Never
   flush oil toward a floor drain and never wash oil near a live panel.
4. Bag the used absorbent in the yellow waste drum. It is not general waste.
5. Record the spill and the cause on the shift sheet.

## 2. Large spill, running leak, or spray

Leave the area, raise the alarm, and call the plant emergency line from outside
the cell. A pressurised hydraulic leak can inject fluid through skin; a fine
spray is treated as an emergency even if nobody feels an injury.

## 3. Fluid on skin or in eyes

Coolant on skin: wash with soap and water. Coolant in eyes: eye wash station at
the cell entrance for 15 minutes, then report. Any suspected fluid injection
injury goes straight to hospital and is reported the same hour.

## 4. After the clean-up

The leak is repaired before the machine runs again. Topping the reservoir up and
running the shift out is not a repair.
