# SP-04 Hydraulic Press Guarding and Pinch Points

Northbridge Fabrication, Plant 2. Applies to hydraulic press P-102 on Line 3.

## 1. Guarding devices

P-102 runs with a light curtain across the front opening, fixed side and rear
guards, and two-hand controls that require both hands away from the die area for
the whole closing stroke. The press does not cycle with the light curtain broken.
Each device is function-tested at the start of every shift and the test is
recorded on the shift sheet.

## 2. Pinch points

The die area between the ram and the bed is the principal pinch point. Secondary
pinch points: the blank feed chute, the scrap chute, the part ejector, and the
gap between the conveyor C-7 take-off and the press bed.

## 3. Hands in the die area

No part of the body enters the die area while the machine is capable of motion.
Parts and scrap are removed with the hook tool, never by hand through the light
curtain. A part that cannot be reached with the hook tool is a lockout job under
SP-01.

## 4. Stored energy

The ram can move with the pump off. Before any work in the die area, the
accumulator is bled to zero and the ram is lowered to the bed or blocked, as part
of SP-01.

## 5. Failed device

If the light curtain, a two-hand control, or a guard interlock fails its test or
fails during a shift, the press is taken out of service and locked out. The press
does not run in a reduced mode while the device is on order.
