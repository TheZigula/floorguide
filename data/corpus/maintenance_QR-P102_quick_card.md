# QR-P102 Press P-102 Quick-Reference Card

Laminated card at the P-102 panel. Printed 2024-03-11 from MM-P102 Rev A.
This card is a copy for quick lookup. The manual is the controlled source.

## Service at a glance

| Item | Interval |
|------|----------|
| Hydraulic filter | every 500 hours |
| Seal inspection | every 500 hours |
| Hydraulic oil | every 1000 hours |

## Shift checks

Oil level at the sight glass. Leaks under the bed. Light curtain test.
Note the meter reading on the shift sheet.

## Noise at the pump

Check the oil level, then call maintenance.

Card printed 2024-03-11. Check MM-P102 for current intervals before scheduling.
