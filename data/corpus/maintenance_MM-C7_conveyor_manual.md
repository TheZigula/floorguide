# MM-C7 Conveyor C-7 Maintenance Manual

Northbridge Fabrication, Plant 2, Line 3. Rev B, issued 2026-03-30. C-7 is the
take-off conveyor between press P-102 and the inspection bench.

## 2. Service intervals

Intervals are in belt running hours from the meter reading at the last service of
that item.

| Item | Interval | Reference |
|------|----------|-----------|
| Head and tail bearings, lubricate | every 200 running hours | Sec 3.1 |
| Belt tension, check and adjust | every 500 running hours | Sec 3.3 |
| Belt tracking, check | every 500 running hours | Sec 3.4 |
| Drive chain, inspect and lubricate | every 1000 running hours | Sec 3.5 |

### 3.1 Bearing lubrication

Lubricate the head and tail bearings every 200 running hours with two shots of
NLGI 2 lithium grease per fitting, no more. Over-greasing pushes the seal out and
lets chips into the race. Record the meter reading in the service log.

### 3.3 Belt tension

Correct tension is the lowest tension that carries the load without slip at the
drive pulley. Over-tensioning is the usual cause of early bearing failure on C-7.
A bearing that has been lubricated on schedule and still runs hot is a tension
problem, not a lubrication problem.

## 4. Symptoms and first checks

Squeal at start-up: check tension before adding grease. Belt drifting to one
side: check tracking and the tail pulley squareness. Hot bearing housing: stop
the conveyor, lock out under SP-01, and check both tension and lubrication.

## 5. Clearing a jam

A jam is never cleared with the conveyor capable of motion, and never by reaching
into the chip trench. Lockout under SP-01 first; trench entry is a permit job
under SP-05.
