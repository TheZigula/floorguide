# QC-02 In-Process Inspection Frequency

Northbridge Fabrication, Plant 2, Line 3. Applies to every part run on Line 3,
including the B-40 bracket.

## 1. Standard frequency

1. First piece at every setup, measured in full against QC-01 and recorded on the
   setup sheet before the run is released.
2. Then one piece in every 50 for the rest of the run, measured on the
   characteristics listed on the control plan.
3. Last piece of the run, measured in full and recorded.

## 2. When the count restarts at first piece

The run is treated as a new setup, and the first-piece check is done again,
after any of these:

- a tooling change, an insert change, or a die change;
- an offset or program edit at the machine;
- a machine fault, an emergency stop, or any unplanned stop under SP-03;
- a maintenance intervention that touched the tooling, the fixture, or the axes;
- a material lot change;
- a shift handover mid-run.

## 3. Tightened frequency

After a nonconformance is raised under QC-03, the frequency tightens to one piece
in every 10 for the next 100 parts. It returns to one in 50 only when those 100
parts are all in tolerance and the quality engineer records the return.

## 4. Records

Every measurement is recorded with the part count, the reading, the gage id, and
the initials of the person who took it. An unrecorded check did not happen.
