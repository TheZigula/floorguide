# QC-01 Dimensional Tolerance, Bracket B-40

Northbridge Fabrication, Plant 2, Line 3. Controlled quality standard for the
B-40 bracket, pressed on P-102 and machined on M-31.

## 1. Characteristics and tolerances

| Characteristic | Nominal | Tolerance | Limits |
|----------------|---------|-----------|--------|
| Overall length | 120.00 mm | plus or minus 0.15 mm | 119.85 mm to 120.15 mm |
| Hole centre spacing | 60.00 mm | plus or minus 0.10 mm | 59.90 mm to 60.10 mm |
| Hole diameter | 10.50 mm | plus 0.05 mm, minus 0.00 mm | 10.50 mm to 10.55 mm |
| Flange angle | 90.0 degrees | plus or minus 0.5 degrees | 89.5 to 90.5 degrees |
| Flatness of the base face | - | 0.20 mm | 0.20 mm maximum |

The overall length of the B-40 bracket is 120.00 mm with a tolerance of plus or
minus 0.15 mm. A part measuring outside 119.85 mm to 120.15 mm is out of
tolerance.

## 2. Measurement conditions

Parts are measured at 20 degrees C after they have cooled from the machining
operation. A part measured hot reads long. Overall length and hole spacing are
measured with a calibrated digital caliper or on the bench CMM; the gage carries
a current calibration label (QC-04).

## 3. Out of tolerance

A part outside the limits is not reworked at the machine and is not passed on for
the next operation. It is hold-tagged and segregated under QC-03, and the
inspection frequency returns to first-piece under QC-02 once the cause has been
corrected.

## 4. Drawing

Drawing B-40 sheet 1 Rev 4 is the master. Where this standard and the drawing
differ, the drawing is corrected and reissued; production stops on that
characteristic until they agree.
