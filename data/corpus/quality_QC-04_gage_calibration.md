# QC-04 Gage Calibration and Measurement Equipment

Northbridge Fabrication, Plant 2, Line 3.

## 1. Calibration intervals

| Equipment | Interval |
|-----------|----------|
| Digital calipers | every 6 months |
| Micrometers | every 6 months |
| Bore gages and setting rings | every 12 months |
| Bench CMM | every 12 months, plus a daily artefact check |
| Torque wrenches | every 12 months |

Every gage carries a calibration label with its id and the date it is next due.
A gage with no label, an expired label, or an unreadable label is not used.

## 2. Daily checks

The bench CMM is checked each morning against the reference artefact before the
first measurement of the shift, and the reading is recorded. A check outside
tolerance takes the CMM out of service until it is recalibrated.

## 3. A gage found out of calibration

When a gage is found out of calibration, every measurement it took since its last
good check is suspect. Those parts are recalled and re-inspected with a calibrated
gage, back to the last good check, and anything outside QC-01 is hold-tagged under
QC-03.

## 4. Damaged gages

A dropped caliper or micrometer is taken out of use the same shift and sent for
recalibration, whatever the label says. Damage does not wait for the due date.
