# QC-03 Nonconformance and Hold-Tag Procedure

Northbridge Fabrication, Plant 2, Line 3. What happens when a part, a lot, or a
process output does not meet its standard.

## 1. Raising a nonconformance

Anyone on the line raises a nonconformance. It is raised the moment the condition
is seen, not at the end of the run. The trigger is any measurement outside the
limits in QC-01, any visible defect, or any doubt about whether a part meets the
standard. Doubt counts.

## 2. Hold tag and segregation

1. Stop the operation on that characteristic.
2. Attach a red hold tag to the part or to the container, filled in with the part
   number, the lot, the count, the date, the characteristic in question, and who
   raised it.
3. Move the held material to the marked hold area at the end of Line 3. Held
   material does not sit at the machine, where it gets mixed back in.
4. Quarantine the suspect population back to the last good check under QC-02, not
   just the one part that was measured.

## 3. Disposition

Only the quality engineer dispositions held material, and the disposition is
recorded before anything moves: use as is, rework to a written plan, or scrap.
Nobody on the floor releases held material, and a hold tag is never removed by
the person who raised it.

## 4. Cause and return to run

The cause is recorded before the line returns to normal frequency. The run
restarts at first piece under QC-02 and stays at the tightened frequency until
the quality engineer records the return.
